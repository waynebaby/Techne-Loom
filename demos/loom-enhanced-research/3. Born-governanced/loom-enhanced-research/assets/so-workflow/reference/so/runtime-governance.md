## Governance Facts

- Every transition needs executable guard and success evidence plus explicit ownership.
- Boundary checks must happen on the same external workflow copy before the next step.
- A gate needs machine-checkable evidence and a route-aware completion or blocked outcome.
- A blocked result is a weave-out that needs a structured continuation; it is not completion evidence.
